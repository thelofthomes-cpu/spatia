FAQ

Common questions

What capture equipment do I need?

Nothing specialized. Use a 360° camera, an iPhone with LiDAR, or a regular phone/camera for photogrammetry — SPATIA supports all three.

How accurate is the AI assistant?

SPATIA AI answers from the property's actual spatial model and the listing data you provide, so responses stay grounded in the real property rather than guessing.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes — Pro and Enterprise workspaces can publish tours under a custom domain and match your brand's colors and logo.

How long does a property take to go live?

Most single-unit properties are captured, processed and published within a day. Larger properties with more rooms may take a little longer.

Is there a contract?

No. Starter is free, Pro is billed monthly and can be cancelled any time. Enterprise plans are agreed per contract.

Do buyers need to install an app?

No — tours run in any modern browser on desktop or mobile. Nothing to install for the people viewing your listings.
